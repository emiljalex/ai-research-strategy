Bring a live product commitment and the person entitled to change it. On one sheet, write two alternatives the organization could still afford to choose. If the team cannot name a second option, first ask what the current study could change about scope, sequence or exposure.

## Write the two futures

Imagine a parts-return service. One proposal funds a clearer request form. Another lets a smaller group of supervisors waive an eligibility rule during a pilot. Both could reduce the number of abandoned returns. They would change different parts of the service and put different people in charge of exceptions.

Write a sentence for each alternative that names who would do something differently. Ask the owner to cross out any option already foreclosed by contract, staffing, or budget. Record the last date when the remaining choice can change.

## Specify what would move the money

Complete this sentence separately for each alternative. **If we observe ___ among ___, the owner will reconsider ___ before ___.** For the form, look for eligible requesters who cannot complete it. For the rule, look for people who complete it accurately and still cannot get a reasonable exception. Include the staff expected to honor the request.

Use machine assistance to retrieve passages and propose rival explanations for review. Keep each source attached to its interpretation. Give a researcher responsibility for asking whose experience is absent. Give the budget owner responsibility for stating whether the observation would actually change the decision.

## Leave a record of disagreement

At the commitment meeting, keep the disposition short. Which alternative did the owner choose, for which population, on what evidence? What objection survived? Who can reopen the choice, and what would justify the cost?

Retain an explicit empty field for questions the project cannot answer. A question about who writes an eligibility rule may deserve its own investigation even if the next form release cannot address it. Record who will take it up and when.

## Try the smallest version

Use this sheet once, before commissioning a study. Return after the commitment and compare the owner’s account with the initial brief. If the study could never have moved the named choice, record when it closed and what the research did achieve. This is a proposed working method. Its value in a real organization still has to be tested.
