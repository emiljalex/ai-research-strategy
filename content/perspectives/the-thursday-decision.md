Suppose two research teams deliver exactly the same finding. A new self-service form works for people who meet its eligibility rules. People just outside those rules have to start again with a supervisor. Improving the form would make the designed path easier to use. It would leave a separate class of failed requests untouched.

The first team reports on Wednesday. A product leader can still move one engineer from the form redesign to a trial of delegated exceptions. The second team reports on Friday. On Thursday the organization signed an agreement that fixes the engineering work for the quarter.

The two teams could produce identical reports, with equally strong evidence, and do different work in the world. The difference is the point at which the organization can use what they learned.

## Buy access to the choice

A research budget pays for interviews, observations, analysis and judgment. It can also buy the opportunity to investigate a question while alternatives are still live. That second purchase requires an organizational commitment. A decision owner must say what is open, when it closes, and who can act if the evidence favors an inconvenient option.

An AI assistant might shorten the interval between the interview and the report. It cannot, by itself, reopen Thursday’s agreement. If the research function receives its assignments only after implementation has been scoped, more production capacity can leave its strategic reach exactly where it was.

## Give the Friday finding a future

The Friday team still learned something worth keeping. It identified a class of people the service excludes. That observation could become a question about delegated authority, affect the next contract, or change whom the organization recruits for a subsequent study.

Give it an address. Record the rule under dispute, the roles it affects, and the next occasion when the organization can revise it. Name someone who can challenge that account later. Otherwise the person who remembers an inconvenient finding becomes its only path back into a decision.

This is a thought experiment about timing and authority. A useful test would follow actual commitments through planning, including occasions when the study supported the original choice.
